🛕 About the Temple 🛕

The ancient and famous temple, named after its five chambers, is a sacred place where Sri Bhadra Bhagavathi, Sri Maha Vishnu, Sri Maha Ganapathi and other deities shower blessings on devotees. Annual special rituals and poojas bring the community together in devotion, making it a place of faith, peace and divine grace.

🛕 Special Poojas 🛕

  1. Karyasidhi Ponkala – Conducted every Tuesday.
  2. Ayilya Pooja – Every month on the day of Ayilyam.
  3. Manchirat Nercha – Every Friday.
  4. Ramayanaparayanam – From the first day of Karkidakam.
  5. Mankalathil Pongala – On the day of Chothi in the month of Meenam.
  6. Naagarootu – On the day of Ayilyam in the month of Kanni.
